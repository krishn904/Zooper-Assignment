**Zopper Business Intelligence Assignment**

This repository contains the solution for the Business Intelligence internship assignment. The objective of this assignment was to analyze insurance policy sales and claims data using SQL and derive meaningful business insights from the dataset. The project demonstrates skills in data analysis, SQL querying, and data visualization.

---

**Project Objective**

The main objective of this assignment is to analyze insurance datasets to extract useful business insights. Using SQL queries and Excel-based visualizations, the project aims to understand policy sales performance, claims patterns, and overall business trends.

---

**Datasets Used**

Two datasets were used in this assignment:

Policy Sales Dataset  
This dataset contains information related to insurance policies sold by the company. It includes details such as policy ID, policy type, premium amount, region, and policy start dates.

Claims Dataset  
This dataset contains information about insurance claims made by customers. It includes details such as claim ID, policy ID, claim date, claim amount, and claim status.

These datasets were used to analyze the relationship between policy sales and claims.

**Project Workflow**

1. Data Understanding  
The datasets were examined to understand their structure, columns, and relationships.

2. Data Querying Using SQL  
SQL queries were written to answer the analytical questions provided in the assignment.

3. Business Analysis  
The query results were analyzed to extract useful insights related to policy sales and claims.

4. Data Visualization  
Excel charts were created to visually represent trends such as monthly claims.

5. Documentation  
All SQL queries, insights, and explanations were documented in this repository.

---

**SQL Analysis**

Six SQL queries were written to answer analytical questions related to the dataset. These queries include calculations such as total premium collected, claim trends, and policy analysis.

Each query is written with a clear explanation of the logic used and the expected output.

---

**Business Insights**

Based on the analysis of the datasets, several important business insights were identified:

Claims tend to vary across different months, indicating seasonal claim patterns.

Certain policy types generate higher claim frequencies compared to others.

Premium revenue helps understand how much income is generated from policies.

Analyzing claim trends helps companies predict potential future risks and manage finances better.

Understanding the relationship between policies and claims helps improve business strategy and risk management.

---

**Data Visualization**

An Excel dashboard was created to visually represent the trends found in the data. The dashboard includes charts such as:

Monthly claim trends  
Policy sales distribution  
Claim amount analysis  

These visualizations help in quickly understanding the overall performance of the insurance business.
